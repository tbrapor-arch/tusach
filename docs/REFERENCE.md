# Reference: formats and tools

## Layout
```
course/lessons/NN.yaml   lessons (none yet: the syllabus and word list wait for the owner's approval); nothing else
                         may be there (lint); the template is course/lesson_template.yaml
course/syllabus.yaml     the plan and the owner's approvals (lint refuses lessons without them)
course/lexicon.tsv       the course lexicon: one row per sense taught (English, origin, characters, etymon, false-friend
                         note, usage, confidence sure|likely|doubt, source, override); Claude's, NOT native-checked;
                         wins on the card (layer 1, below); columns and checks in tools/lexicon.py
course/example-block.tsv Tatoeba sentence ids no card shows (mistranslation, typo, proverb), each with its reason
course/proposal/         syllabus proposal, word list 1 (11 columns, regenerated by tools/wordlist.py, sense tags), its
                         to-check list (the lexicon's doubts and overrides: tools/wordlist.py --to-check)
tests/golden/            cards.tsv (expected card fields of every lexicon row and the top 300) and opaque-sample.tsv
                         (review 2's sample of opaque analyses judged T/O): tools/test_golden.py, in `make test`
tools/                   build, pipeline, checks (MIT)
tools/fixtures/          TEST FIXTURE: dict.yaml (invented entries), lessons/00.yaml (3 lines), raw/ (a kaikki-shaped
                         file), raw-real/ (27 real Wiktionary lines, CC BY-SA, for tools/test_db.py); not content
tests/cutter/            reference.tsv: 200 sentences written and cut by Claude (docs/SEGMENTATION.md; rules tuned on it);
                         heldout.tsv: 100 Tatoeba sentences (id, author) cut by Claude, no rule tuned on them; NOT
                         native-checked
docs/SEGMENTATION.md     the word-boundary convention (lessons, the cutter reference, vi.cut()'s rules)
data/fetch.sh            open sources pinned by content (ATTRIBUTION.md); a missing one fails (TUSACH_ALLOW_MISSING)
data/fetch-lib.sh        its functions: fetch (clean git checkout), get (SHA-256), snap (our release), npmpkg
data/snapshot-request.txt  a commit changing it runs .github/workflows/snapshot.yml (tools/pin_snapshot.py TAG)
data/build/              generated, never committed: vi.db, vi-nc.db, cards.json
requirements-build*.txt  build/test packages (wordfreq; underthesea only for the cutter report), locked with hashes
app/                     page template, recording page, PWA files, embedded font; app/dist/ is generated
app/published-*.{txt,tsv}  card ids and English (rank and text) of published pages (committed, only ever grow)
app/english-changes.tsv  a published gloss shortened on purpose: card id, new English, reason (else an error)
```

## Lesson format (`course/lessons/NN.yaml`, file name = id)
```yaml
id: 1
title: {vi: Chào anh, en: Hello}
review: false                      # true on every 7th lesson
setting: One line of scene setting, in English.
native_check: {by: name, date: 2026-12-01}   # omit until a native speaker has checked it; the app then says so
dialogue:
- {n: 1, speaker: Lan, vi: "Chào anh! Tôi là học_sinh.", en: "Hello! I'm a student."}
notes:
- {ref: 1, topic: bridge, text: "học sinh is 學生 …"}      # ref 0 = recap note; topic free (bridge, pronouns, false-friend…)
new_words:
- {w: học_sinh, en: "pupil, student"}                       # optional: tag, chars, label (same | false-friend | native)
- {w: Lan, en: "Lan (a woman's name)", name: true}
exercises:
  translate: [{vi: "Anh là học_sinh.", en: "You are a student."}]
  fill: [{vi: "Chị ___ là học_sinh.", answer: cũng, en: "You are a student too."}]
```
**Word boundaries** (decided 17 Nov 2026): underscores join the syllables of one word (`học_sinh`, `Hà_Nội`); spaces
separate words. Every space-separated piece is one word, so the course never depends on the cutter. A homograph takes
`~tag` (`đường~road`), defined by a dictionary entry's tag or a `new_words` entry with that tag; the tag is not shown.
Numbers are not words. Names are declared once in any lesson's `new_words` with `name: true`.
**Tone-mark placement** (house style, decided 18 Nov 2026): the traditional one, as in most print and in the subtitle
counts (khỏe 5,853 against khoẻ 957 in FrequencyWords): in an open rhyme of two vowels the mark sits on the first
(hòa, khỏe, thủy, mùa), else on the vowel with a diacritic, else on the last vowel before a coda (hoàn), else the middle
of three (ngoái). `vi.key()` writes every syllable this way, so hoà and hòa find the same card; the line keeps its own
spelling on screen; lint asks for the house style (error). quí/quý are different spellings, not placements.
**Spelling groups** (rewritten 18 Nov 2026 after review 2): only the SPELLINGS of one word share a key, card and rank:
i/y after a consonant or qu (lí do / lý do, kĩ thuật / kỹ thuật, Mĩ / Mỹ, quí / quý), hyphens (tiến-hoá / tiến hoá; a
hyphenated word alone, sô-cô-la, is filed as sô cô la) and letter case; tone-mark placement is one key anyway. The
**house spelling** is the spelling without hyphens, then the most frequent (wordfreq phrase estimate, English leakage
removed; ties to Wiktionary's main entry): the y spelling in most i/y groups (công ty, kỹ thuật, lý do, Mỹ), the i
spelling in a few (bác sĩ, nghệ sĩ, bánh mì). Lint asks for it (error); cards and the cutter find every spelling
(`cards.canon`). NOT pooled (finding 1: ga had become ra, ấy ý, dậy vậy, nhỉ nhờ): "alternative form of" (cảm ơn /
cám ơn, tui / tôi: a word of its own, with its target's characters, origin and English), and pronunciation /
eye-dialect spellings (`alt_kind` pron: ga of ra, í of ấy, biếu of bướu): their own key, senses and rank; the card shows
their cross-reference only, among the other etymologies.
**Sense tags**: `~tag` names a course lexicon row (`từ~from`, `năm~year`, `phải~must`), else a part of speech
(`phải~verb`) or a word of exactly one etymology's English (`cards.sense_tag`). A word whose key has several lexicon
senses, or (no lexicon row) several etymologies, must be written with its tag (lint error, never a silent first card).
A character taught without a word entry: `tam~三` (the `character-reading` entries, never ranked).
**Syllabus plan** (`course/syllabus.yaml` lessons): `{n, en, vi, review, grammar, bridge, strand, lines, new_words:
{native, hv}}`; lint warns when a written lesson leaves it by more than one (lines, new words) or 0.2 (Hán-Việt share).
The approved word list (`approved.word_list.file`) is an 11-column TSV (`course.WORDLIST_COLS`); `lint.py --wordlist
FILE` checks one alone. A bridge note's chữ Hán must be the same word in Chinese for the sense taught: a lexicon row with these characters,
origin hv or hv-gram, not doubtful, not a false friend (唐 is đường~road's analysis, doubtful: refused); with no
lexicon row, a database word labelled same/gram; and all four readings.
**Syllable table** (`tools/vi.py`, finding 10): built from a stated description of the phonotactics (nuclei with
their codas, the medial /w/, qu and gi), not a word list; checked at each build against every Thiều Chửu reading and
the FrequencyWords syllables seen 200+ times (`build_db.py validate_syllables`, result in HISTORY).
Chữ Hán never appear in `vi` lines or titles; notes may use them (bridge notes). Quote any text with a comma in a
flow mapping (`{w: tôi, en: "I, me"}`): lint refuses unknown keys, which is what an unquoted comma makes. Exercises
must be new: lint refuses one equal to, contained in, or equal after a greeting/first clause to a dialogue line, and
warns at a similarity of 0.8.

## Word card (`DATA.dict[id]`, built only by `tools/cards.py`)
Id: the word's key (lower-case NFC, spaces between syllables), `~tag` for a homograph, `name:` + surface for a name.
Fields: `w`, `k`, `en`, `en_src` (course | lexicon | dict:SOURCE | parts), `en_rank` (3/3/2/1/0), `chars`, `hv`
(Hán-Việt per character, labelled), `yue` (Jyutping), `nan` (Tâi-lô LITERARY reading), `nan_coll` (the MOE word
reading when it is not the literary one: "colloquial / everyday"), `cmn` (pinyin), `by_char`, `label`, `ex {vi, en}`,
`other` (other senses / etymologies of the spelling: en, chars), `parts` (syllable cards), `rank`, `audio`, `name`,
`fixture`, `src`, `origin` (in words), `doubt` (why doubtful/opaque), `etymon` (an older loan's characters with their
readings), `per_char` (formed in Vietnamese: each character's readings, no composed "Chinese word"), `nan_flag`
(colloquial only), `ff` (false-friend note), `use` (usage note), `conf` (lexicon confidence), `no_main` (several
etymologies, none attested as the common one), `ety` (the dictionary etymology used: source and Wiktionary number).
**Three layers.** (1) The course lexicon (`course/lexicon.tsv`, `tools/lexicon.py`): when it has the card id (or, for an
untagged word, the key: its first row is the main sense) it decides English (`en_src` lexicon, "written by Claude, not
checked by a native speaker" on the card), the sense, the origin label (hv same | hv-gram gram | hv-opaque opaque |
hv-vn | older-loan | french/loan loan | native | compound | unknown "no etymology recorded"), characters, etymon and
false-friend note; confidence doubt on a Chinese origin makes the label doubtful. Readings are joined from the
database on its characters. The lexicon row is matched to a database etymology (characters, etymon, first sense, the
only one) for the example and the other senses. Each row is validated against the data (`tools/lexicon.py`:
no-key, hv-mismatch, origin-conflict, chars-conflict, en-unsupported, not-attested, bad-row); lint fails on a conflict
the row does not override with a reason. (2) The dictionary for every other word (below). (3) The golden check
(`tools/test_golden.py`) and the real-card compare (`tools/compare.py`).
English, characters, label and example of a card come from ONE etymology. Shown top to bottom: word and ▶, English
(+ its source and confidence), usage, chữ Hán with Cantonese / Taigi / Mandarin side by side, the label, the example.
**Examples** (`cards.choose_example`, review 2 finding 6): Tatoeba candidates attached to the word as a WHOLE word by
the cutter (40 per key; flagged and never shown: profanity/sex/drugs, slashes, a word no dictionary has; and the ids of
`course/example-block.tsv`), the closest to 8 syllables first; the first whose linked translation fits the sense (a
word of the card's English, none that only another sense of the spelling has; numbers, days and time words of the
sentence present in the translation); a word with no other sense takes the first aligned one; a lexicon function word
(của "of") the first without another sense's words; else none (no example rather than a wrong one).

## Database (`tools/schema.sql`, built by `tools/build_db.py`)
`meta` (nc, sources, missing, freq_method), `source`, `entry` (word, key, english, chars, origin
sino|nonhv|vietic|loan|compound|'', origin_note, etymon, alt_of/alt_kind, hv_check, sino_kind word|vn, doubt
doubtful|opaque, label, en_score, freq per billion, freq_rank), `sense` (each Wiktionary sense with its tags),
`translation` / `translation_zh` (English Wiktionary tables), `spelling` (key -> house key), `cedict_en`, `example` (ref: Tatoeba id and author), `hanviet`
(char, reading, kind hv|nom|hannom|unchecked, source, checked against the other Hán-Việt source, label, use), `chinese`
(chars, lang yue|nan|cmn, reading, level word|char, share, note), `variant` (Unihan), `bridge` (one row per chữ Hán
spelling of the entries, with `via`).
Loaders (`SOURCES`): wiktionary (kaikki: words, English without misspelling senses and archaic ones last;
`vi-etym-sino` → chars, or origin `nonhv` + etymon for "Non-Sino-Vietnamese reading of X"; several templates =
alternatives; character readings by tag; "Sino-Vietnamese reading of X" entries → readings; real format in HISTORY),
thieuchuu (Hán-Việt per character; `Ð`→`Đ`; heads with a stroke count `đại (5 n)` read), unihan (kVietnamese,
`unchecked`; kMandarin → `mandarin_pref`; kSemanticVariant/kZVariant → `variant`), rime (Jyutping; chars with rime's %,
words), moe (Tâi-lô: a character's literary 文讀=1 readings, its unmarked 0 ones only when it has no literary one;
words' own readings; accent variants `a/b` split; appendix types skipped),
cedict (pinyin, traditional forms only, numbers → marks with the apostrophe and syllabic m/n/ng; capitalised =
`name`), tatoeba (examples with id and author, attached to the words vi.cut() finds, 2 per word, 4-20 syllables closest
to 8; no orphans, no line that is not Vietnamese: `tatoeba_problem`), and the
Tatoeba Challenge test set (corpus for the counts only). A missing file: "source X not fetched", `meta.missing`.
- **Bridge join** (`tools/bridge.py join()`, the same for cards, the `bridge` table and coverage): Cantonese and
  Mandarin: the whole word if the source has it, else character by character with every reading in brackets, main
  first (`hok6 (saang1/sang1)`, `by_char` on the card, `*` in the Bridge tab), else missing. Taigi: the column is the
  LITERARY reading (the layer that matches Hán-Việt), composed from the characters' literary readings; it is
  "attested" when the MOE word entry reads the same; a different MOE word reading (生日 senn-ji̍t, 先生 sian-sinn) is
  shown apart as "colloquial / everyday", never as literary. Mandarin order: Unihan kMandarin's reading first, then
  the number of CC-CEDICT words using the reading; surname/place readings dropped when there are others.
- **Variant characters**: when the word's own characters give no reading, the same word with Unihan semantic/Z
  variants (行爲 → 行為, 祕密 → 秘密) is tried; `bridge.via` and the card name the spelling used.
- **Hán-Việt** (`tools/hanviet.py`): Northern standard first, the reading most Sino-Vietnamese words use first
  (`hanviet.use`); readings attested by 2+ Wiktionary Sino-Vietnamese words (or 1 for an unread character) are
  Hán-Việt (`attested`: 實 thực); Thiều Chửu's Southern forms (sanh, chánh, võ, nhựt, bổn, ngãi…) come
  after, labelled "Southern variant"; where only the Southern form exists the Northern one is derived by the rule
  table (label "derived"); a character no Hán-Việt source reads gets Unihan's readings of Hán-Việt shape, labelled
  "unchecked, Unihan"; else "— (no Hán-Việt reading)", listed by `make lint` and `make coverage`. A Wiktionary
  analysis whose characters do not match the syllables (`hv_check` differs/length; hyphens split syllables, kì = kỳ)
  is not shown as "same word"; `make lint` lists it.
- **Etymologies on a card** (`cards.senses`, finding 3): (source, etymology number, characters, origin). The main one
  is the etymology the corpus shows in use (`build_db.etymology_usage`): each Tatoeba sentence with the word counts for
  an etymology whose senses have a word of its translation that no other etymology has (bracketed explanations, "is",
  form-of and late senses left out); at least 3 sentences and twice the next one, else the evidence is weak: page
  order, and the card says that no main sense is claimed (`no_main`). A page whose first etymology is a usage
  description (các "Plural marker") cannot be counted: page order. Names, character readings and pronunciation
  spellings come last.
- **Labels** (`build_db.etymology_states`, finding 4, one per etymology): `same` (Hán-Việt, the same word in Chinese:
  one character, or a word of CC-CEDICT / rime / the MOE / a Chinese row of a translation table, also under a Unihan
  variant spelling, names included: 臨安) | `gram` (one character whose match with CC-CEDICT is through function words:
  the same morpheme in a grammatical use, 如 như, 自 tự) | `hv-vn` (no Chinese source has the word: 關重 quan trọng) |
  `doubtful` (Wiktionary doubts the source in the sentence that gives it, or offers another source, and the sense test
  does not confirm it) | `opaque` (a character rare in CC-CEDICT, variants counted; or ONE character none of whose
  senses shares a word, with light stemming and synonyms, with any CC-CEDICT sense of it or its variants, nor a
  translation table with it) | `older-loan` | `loan` | `native` | `compound` (Wiktionary's compound, reduplication or
  calque templates, "X + Y" texts, or syllables that all have an origin) | '' unknown, shown "no etymology recorded"
  (with "(not Chinese)" when no etymology of the spelling has characters). A language code no table names is shown by
  the template's own language name (sit -> Sino-Tibetan, omx -> Old Mon). `false-friend` from the lexicon only.
  Measured on review 2's sample (`tests/golden/opaque-sample.tsv`): false opaque 53 of 73 at fa4d343, 7 of 74 now
  (孤 炒 縣 樣 依 眾 奧), false same 0.
- **Card English** (`english.compose`, finding 2): Wiktionary's own sense order within an entry; misspellings and
  "alternative form of" senses beside real ones dropped; late senses (archaic, obsolete, rare, slang, vulgar,
  dialectal, Southern, humorous…; a Northern-tagged sense is ordinary) after the others; a sense longer than 60
  characters only as the first item (then without its brackets); a trailing "compare …"/"(see …)" removed; a sense's
  own "; " becomes ", " so no fragment is shown; at most 4 items and 100 characters. No translation-table scoring:
  the English Wiktionary's tables are evidence only (between etymologies through `en_score`, the lexicon's validation,
  and `make coverage`'s "missing-main" report). Of a nested Wiktionary sense, the general gloss when short (với "with").
- **Taigi colloquial-only** (finding 8): an unmarked MOE character reading whose coda class differs from every
  Hán-Việt reading (-h for a stop, -nn for a nasal…) is colloquial (`chinese.note`), never literary: 167 characters,
  149 with no other reading (冊 tsheh, 院 īnn, 醬 tsiùnn); the literary column is then empty. Agreeing classes: m/n,
  p/t, k/t, a nasal vowel with an open rhyme (他 thann, 且 tshiánn, 打 tánn: nasalisation is in both layers) and -in
  with -ng (孕 īn, 拯 tsín). MOE-marked literary readings are kept. Blind spots, listed by `make coverage`: a colloquial
  reading of the same coda class (條 tiâu beside liâu; 270 characters have 2+ unmarked readings that all agree), a
  colloquial nasalised open syllable (鼻 phīnn), an irregular Hán-Việt reading (呆 ngốc, 炸 tạc). The card says that
  the layer is this app's classification where the MOE marks none.
- **Frequency** (`tools/freq.py`): each syllable's frequency (wordfreq less its English leakage: the share the English
  word of the same letters explains, k = 0.0073; else FrequencyWords) is divided among the dictionary words containing
  it in the proportions counted in a corpus cut by the project's own cutter (`vi.cut()` with the dictionary's keys:
  Tatoeba snapshot, Wiktionary examples; + the Tatoeba Challenge copy in the private build only), smoothed towards
  wordfreq's own phrase estimate; a word's frequency is the geometric mean over its syllables. Not ranked: keys with
  digits/punctuation, 1-2 letter non-syllables, a common word + a name (tiếng Pháp, người Việt) and keys of 4+
  syllables (bài tập về nhà); a name sharing its key with a lower-case common word (Lan / lan "orchid"), whose
  capitalised uses then do not count for the common word (not Trung Quốc: its other entries are capitalised too).
  **Honest limits**: the corpus is small, written and translated (Tatoeba): spoken particles and food words are
  under-ranked (vâng 3663, ạ 1257, nhỉ 2207, cơm, phở 12139), Southern nha above ạ, Wikipedia-like words over-ranked
  (huyện, Nguyễn), and "in" still carries English leakage. The syllabus, not the rank, decides what is taught.
- **Cutter** (`tools/vi.py cut()`): longest dictionary match, with the rules of docs/SEGMENTATION.md (no join of
  question words, classifier + noun, free function-word sequences, time noun + deictic, verb + complement, word +
  pronoun, numerals, person term + ấy/ta, mọi/mỗi + noun; `LEXICALISED` exceptions).

## The private companion (`tusach-private`)
Same contract as Tsheh-tû's, with neutral names: the app's private-module loader (`Priv` in the page) reads
`module/index.json` (`{"files": {path: sha}}`) through the GitHub API with the sync token (Contents: Read on the
private repo), fetches `module/app.css` and `module/app.js`, caches them by hash, and calls
`window.PrivateModule.init(loader)`, `.route(hash)` (`#private…`, `#books`) and `.fill(root)`. The module adds its
cards with `loader.mergeCards(cards)`: a public card is kept unless the private one has a strictly better English rank
(tested by `tools/smoke.py` against a mocked API). The module may call the page's globals (`openCard`, `closeSheet`,
`esc`, `DATA`, `listSet`, `LIST_TITLES`). Its builds use this repository's `tools/cards.py` with `open_db('private')`,
initialised with the public lessons' `new_words` (same English as the page), their own `cards.Store`, and end with
`check_cards.check()` against their own published files and this repository's `app/published-english.tsv`. The CSP
(`tools/site.py`) allows only `blob:` scripts besides the page's own.
